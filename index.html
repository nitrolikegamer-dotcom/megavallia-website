ABOUT MEGAVALLIA

Connecting creators and audiences.

Megavallia is a social discovery platform built to bring public moments and community voices closer together. Visitors can explore public posts and supported live broadcasts on the website. Creators can use the mobile app to access publishing and other supported creator tools.

Beyond social discovery, Megavallia provides artist promotion information, a free music release planning tool and links to opportunities for creators worldwide.
