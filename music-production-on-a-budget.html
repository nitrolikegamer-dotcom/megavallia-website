Music Production on a Budget: A Beginner's Guide

Learn how to build a simple music production setup, record clean audio and improve your tracks without spending a fortune.

5 lessons25 minutes

What you will learn

  • Choose essential affordable equipment.
  • Prepare a quiet recording space.
  • Record clean vocals and instruments.
  • Edit and arrange a basic track.
  • Export and back up your work.

Course lessons

Lesson 1: Start with what you have

You do not need an expensive studio to begin. A computer or capable phone, headphones and a suitable recording application can help you learn the fundamentals. Identify what your current equipment can do before buying anything.

Task: List your available devices, headphones, microphones and software.

Lesson 2: Create a simple recording space

Choose a quiet room and reduce background noise. Soft furnishings can help reduce reflections. Turn off noisy fans where possible and keep your microphone away from unwanted vibrations.

Task: Record a short test in two different locations and compare the results.

Lesson 3: Record clean audio

Position your microphone consistently and avoid recording too loudly. Leave enough headroom so the signal does not clip. Record several takes and label them clearly.

Task: Record three versions of the same short vocal or instrument part.

Lesson 4: Edit and arrange your track

Remove unwanted silence, organize your recordings and arrange the parts into a clear structure. Learn basic volume adjustment, fades and simple effects. Make changes gradually and compare them with the original recording.

Task: Arrange a short beginning, middle and ending using your recordings.

Lesson 5: Export and protect your project

Export using the format and quality required by your next production step. Keep your original project files, audio recordings and final exports in separate folders. Maintain another backup when possible.

Task: Export one finished demo and create a backup of the project.
