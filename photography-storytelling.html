Photography and Visual Storytelling for Creators

Learn how to use light, composition, perspective and visual details to make more meaningful photographs.

5 lessons25 minutes

What you will learn

  • Understand basic composition.
  • Work with available light.
  • Choose useful camera angles.
  • Tell a story through a photo series.
  • Select and organize your best images.

Course lessons

Lesson 1: Understand composition

Composition is how you arrange elements within a frame. Pay attention to the subject, background, leading lines, balance and empty space. Try different arrangements to see how they affect the viewer's attention.

Task: Photograph one subject using three different compositions.

Lesson 2: Learn to work with light

Notice the direction, softness and color of available light. Window light and open shade can be useful starting points. Avoid harsh light when it creates unwanted shadows, unless that effect is intentional.

Task: Photograph the same subject in soft light and direct light.

Lesson 3: Experiment with perspective

Try photographing from eye level, above and below your subject. Move closer or farther away to change the relationship between the subject and its surroundings. Keep the intended story in mind.

Task: Create three images of one subject from different viewpoints.

Lesson 4: Tell a story with images

A visual story can show a person, place, process or event through a sequence of photographs. Include context, important details and a strong final image. Respect people's privacy and ask permission when appropriate.

Task: Create a five-photo story about a creative activity.

Lesson 5: Edit and present your work

Select images that support your story. Make careful adjustments to exposure, contrast and color without removing important context or misleading viewers. Organize your photographs into a portfolio with useful captions.

Task: Choose five photographs and write a short caption for each.
