How to Release Your First Song Independently

Learn how to prepare your music, choose a distributor, plan your release and promote your first song without needing a record label.

5 lessonsBeginner friendly30 minutes

What you will learn

  • Prepare your final song and artwork.
  • Understand digital music distribution.
  • Prepare your artist profile and release information.
  • Build a simple promotional plan.
  • Review your release after launch.

Course lessons

Lesson 1: Prepare your music

Start with a finished recording. Listen carefully using headphones and speakers. Check for unwanted noise, clipping, incorrect edits and inconsistent volume. Save a high-quality final audio file and keep a separate backup.

Practical task: Create a folder containing your final audio, lyrics, song title, artist name and songwriter credits.

Lesson 2: Create your cover artwork

Your artwork helps listeners recognize your release. Use a clear image, readable design and consistent artist identity. Check the artwork dimensions and content requirements of your chosen distributor before uploading.

Practical task: Prepare one square cover image and check that the title and artist name are easy to read at a small size.

Lesson 3: Understand music distribution

A digital distributor delivers your recording and release information to participating music platforms. Compare distributors carefully. Check their fees, payment terms, supported stores, royalty policies, takedown rules and ownership conditions before choosing one.

Practical task: Compare at least two distributors and write down their fees and terms.

Lesson 4: Plan your release promotion

Prepare short videos, behind-the-scenes material, a short artist introduction and a clear link to your release. Tell your audience when the song is coming and give them a reason to listen. Avoid buying fake streams or followers.

Practical task: Write three social posts: an announcement, a preview and a release-day message.

Lesson 5: Review your release

After launch, check whether your song is available on the platforms selected by your distributor. Review legitimate listener and engagement data. Note which promotional activities brought meaningful attention and use those lessons for your next release.

Practical task: Create a simple report with your release date, links, promotional activities and results.

Your release checklist

  • Final audio file checked and backed up.
  • Artwork prepared according to distributor requirements.
  • Song title, artist details and credits checked.
  • Distributor terms reviewed.
  • Release date and promotion plan prepared.
  • Release links checked after launch.
