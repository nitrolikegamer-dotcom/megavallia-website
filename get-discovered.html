How Independent Creators Can Get Discovered

Learn how to make your creative work easier to find through clear profiles, searchable content, collaboration and consistent promotion.

5 lessons30 minutes

What you will learn

  • Make your creator profile easy to understand.
  • Use descriptive titles and keywords.
  • Build a discoverable portfolio.
  • Collaborate with other creators.
  • Track where discovery comes from.

Course lessons

Lesson 1: Make your profile clear

Use a recognizable name, a clear profile image and a short description of what you create. Include links to your best work. A visitor should quickly understand who you are and what they can explore.

Task: Review your profile and rewrite its description in two clear sentences.

Lesson 2: Help people find your content

Give your work accurate, descriptive titles and captions. Use relevant words that explain the subject, style or location where appropriate. Avoid misleading titles and unrelated tags.

Task: Rewrite three content titles so they clearly describe the work.

Lesson 3: Build a useful portfolio

Collect examples that show the quality and range of your work. Organize them so a visitor can browse easily. Keep your strongest and most current projects easy to access.

Task: Select five pieces for a simple online portfolio.

Lesson 4: Connect and collaborate

Find creators, communities and organizations with related interests. Offer a specific collaboration idea and explain what each person could contribute. Build genuine relationships rather than sending repetitive promotional messages.

Task: Identify three potential collaborators and write one personalized introduction.

Lesson 5: Understand your discovery sources

Review available analytics to see how people reach your work. Compare search, recommendations, shares, referrals and direct visits where those figures are available. Use the information to improve your next project.

Task: Record your main discovery sources once a month.
