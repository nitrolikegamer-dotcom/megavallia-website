Video Creation and Editing for Beginners

Learn the basic steps for planning, recording and editing videos that communicate your ideas clearly.

5 lessons30 minutes

What you will learn

  • Plan a short video.
  • Improve framing and lighting.
  • Record clearer sound.
  • Edit clips into a simple story.
  • Export for your intended platform.

Course lessons

Lesson 1: Plan before recording

Decide what your video should communicate and who it is for. Write a short outline with an opening, main points and ending. A simple plan can reduce unnecessary footage and make editing easier.

Task: Write a five-part outline for a 30-second video.

Lesson 2: Improve framing and lighting

Keep your subject clearly visible and check the background for distractions. Use available natural light where possible. Hold the camera steadily or use a stable support, and choose a frame that suits your intended platform.

Task: Record the same subject using two different camera angles.

Lesson 3: Record clear sound

Audio quality strongly affects how professional a video feels. Record in a quiet place, keep the microphone close enough and listen for wind, echoes or background noise. Capture a short test before recording the full video.

Task: Record a short voice sample and listen back using headphones.

Lesson 4: Edit your story

Arrange clips in a logical order. Remove unnecessary pauses, use simple transitions and make sure text is readable. Music and sound effects should support the message without overpowering speech.

Task: Edit your footage into a short video with a beginning, middle and ending.

Lesson 5: Export and publish

Choose the aspect ratio, resolution and file format suitable for your destination. Review the exported video before publishing. Check captions, spelling, audio levels and any platform-specific requirements.

Task: Export your video and make a final quality checklist.
