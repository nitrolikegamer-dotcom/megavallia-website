Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Megavallia ("Megavallia," "we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you visit our website or use our mobile application. It also explains your privacy choices and rights.

By using Megavallia, you acknowledge this policy. Where consent is legally required, we will request it before processing your information for the relevant purpose.

2. Information We May Collect

  • Account information: Information such as your email address, username, and profile details when you register or use account features in the mobile app.
  • User content: Public posts, videos, captions, comments, profile images, and other content you choose to upload or publish.
  • Usage and technical information: Information such as browser type, device and connection details, pages visited, and diagnostic information, where collected by our services.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: Technologies that may support website functionality, security, analytics, and advertising.
  • Communications: Information you provide when contacting our support team.

The information collected depends on which features you use and which services are enabled.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve Megavallia.
  • Display public posts and other content you choose to publish.
  • Provide account, communication, and app functionality.
  • Protect the platform, detect abuse, and troubleshoot problems.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.
  • Support advertising and measure ad performance where enabled.

4. Public Content

Content you publish publicly may be visible to other users and visitors to the Megavallia website. Public content may be copied, shared, or captured by others. Do not publish information you intend to keep private.

If you want to remove content or need help with your account, contact us using the support address below. We will review requests in accordance with our platform capabilities and applicable law.

5. Cookies and Google AdSense

Megavallia may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver advertisements, limit repeated ads, measure performance, and, where permitted, personalize advertising.

Advertising cookies and related technologies may be subject to your location, consent choices, and Google's policies. The availability and use of advertising features depend on the configuration of our website and advertising account.

You can review Google's advertising settings at Google Ads Settings. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website features.

For more information, review Google's advertising technologies information and Google's Privacy Policy.

We may rely on third-party providers to operate Megavallia, such as hosting, database, authentication, streaming, payment, and advertising providers, when those services are enabled. We do not describe public content as confidential once you choose to publish it publicly. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, our services, or our legal rights.

We use service providers and reasonable safeguards intended to protect information. However, no method of online transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by data type and feature.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

To make a privacy request, contact support@megavallia.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Megavallia is not intended for children where their use would violate applicable age requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter.

Our service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be applied to such processing.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Megavallia develops or our legal obligations change. We will publish the updated policy on this page and revise the "Last updated" date.

12. Contact Us

For questions, concerns, or privacy requests, contact: support@megavallia.com
