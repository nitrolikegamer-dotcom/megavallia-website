Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Megavallia ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our website or use our mobile application and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

2. Information We Collect

When you use Megavallia, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address and username provided when you sign in or register through our app.
  • User Content: Public posts, videos, captions, and comments you choose to share publicly.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and cookies to ensure smooth functionality and security.

3. Advertising and Google AdSense

We may display advertisements on our website provided by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Data Security

We use secure cloud infrastructure (Supabase) to store user authentication and public feed data securely. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@megavallia.com
